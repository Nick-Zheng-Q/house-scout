# Third-party sources / 第三方来源

The source repository distributes original instructions, helpers, tests and concise, attributed demo facts—not copied listing photos, raw web pages, operator PDFs, GIS datasets, browser storage, cookies, GTFS ZIPs or private research archives. Referenced sites retain their rights. Permission to read is not permission to republish.

- Upstream workspace-scout and house-scout Dayweaver Required Notices remain in [LICENSE](LICENSE), PolyForm Shield 1.0.0. No relicensing as MIT/OSI open source.
- OSM-derived routes/coordinates: © OpenStreetMap contributors, [ODbL attribution](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright); [map correction](https://www.openstreetmap.org/). Demo route summaries are limited excerpts, not an OSM database. Check attribution/database obligations for any later redistribution.
- [Cambridge GIS](https://github.com/cambridgegis/cambridgegis_data): public primary address/walkway facts underpin one benchmark; no source GIS files are bundled. Inspect each dataset’s metadata/license before reuse.
- Operator schedules/fares, listing agents and retailer locators: original source URLs and retrieval dates accompany demo facts. No promise that their terms permit production scraping, bulk caching or redistribution. Public results/terms can change; respect authentication, prohibitions, quotas and access denials.
- Optional public Photon/FOSSGIS/Overpass/VBB channels are low-volume research aids, not our infrastructure. Follow their current terms/attribution; no donated service is a guaranteed high-volume backend.
- Packaging/marketing SVGs are original House Scout project artwork, not copied property photos or platform marks. Third-party names identify sources and compatibility, not endorsement.
