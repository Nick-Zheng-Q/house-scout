# Candidate and evidence record

One identifiable unit plus offer per record; do not guess fields to fill a template.

## User requirements and search coverage

- Rent/buy, move-in/completion, household/occupants and routines; city and acceptable areas.
- Each destination, arrival time, monthly trips and usable modes; minimum rooms/area and accessibility/pet/parking needs.
- Monthly housing cap, initial cash, intended term/holding period; daily amenity priorities and acceptable access time.
- Hard versus preferred conditions and decisive questions.
- Search log: **area × platform/channel × query/filter × date × individual units, duplicates, stale/unverified leads**.

## Unit identity and sources

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Listing ID, building/unit, floor, area definition | Publicly necessary precision only |
| Offer, price, dates, publisher and public contact | Rent/buy category, availability, move-in/completion, term |
| Publisher authority and tradeability | Evidenced, unknown, or conflicting; evidence/date |
| Action | Worth viewing / obtain evidence first / not recommended, with decisive reason |

For each link or supplied file, give date, publisher, the unit/offer supported, and source limits. Record whether it is an opened unit detail, search-only text, a building/type page, or an inactive offer, including redirects. For counts, state which of these are included; do not add differently scoped counts as if they were usable homes. Keep duplicate posting conflicts instead of choosing favorable terms. “Posted”, “publisher says available”, “authority/title verified”, and “physically usable” are distinct states. Do not expose unneeded phone numbers or occupant details.

## Conditions and layout

| Decisive condition | Hard/preferred | Met/unmet/unknown/conflicting | Dated evidence | Question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Describe the target unit's independent beds/baths, living/kitchen space, gross/internal area, floor/lift/step-free path, orientation, storage, fixtures, and likely fit for the actual household. Mark generic show-flat plans and unverified claims unknown; list what a visit must check.

## Travel and amenities

For **each** destination: entrance→destination, travel date/time, mode and line/transfers/access walk, door-to-door outbound/return time, one-way/return/monthly fare with trip-count formula, source and limits. Missing fare or return journey is unknown—not free or identical. Distinguish an official general tariff, a published proximity estimate, and a successfully queried route for the actual endpoints/time; “fare retrieved” must identify which scope it means. A named shop with publisher-estimated minutes is not independently verified access. List relevant **named** grocery, pharmacy, healthcare, park, childcare, and commercial areas with actual walk/ride time, purpose, source/date and unverified opening/access. A nearby school/hospital does not confer admission.

## Rental costs or purchase scenario

Rental: itemize normal monthly rent and necessary fees, nonrefundable upfront charges, refundable deposit, prepaid rent, signing due dates, conditional discounts, repair/renewal/exit. Show **known monthly subtotal**, **upfront fees + deposit**, and **cash actually due at signing** separately, each with unknown essentials. If relevant, add *known housing monthly + known essential transport monthly* but never call it a complete total while essentials are missing. Discount expiry matters.

Purchase: separate asking and agreed price, own cash, conditional financing, tax/transaction charges, known repairs, monthly mortgage/maintenance, payment stages, title/co-owner/encumbrance/occupancy/defects. Do not double-count an earnest payment credited toward price. Unknown loan approval, tax, or price means cash and monthly figures are **scenarios** only.

## Next actions

Ask 2–4 questions that would change the decision; identify on-site room, route, amenity, and document checks. Pause payment if identity, authority, payee, tradeability, or serious defects remain in doubt; say which evidence resolves the concern.
