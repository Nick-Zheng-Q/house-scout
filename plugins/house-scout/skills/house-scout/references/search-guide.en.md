# Search and discovery

For fresh searches, start with [named platforms and exact site queries](platforms.en.md). The goal is a few comparable **individual units with current terms**, not an exhaustive city database. For review of supplied material, use this guide only to fill gaps.

## Define search targets

Generate queries from city, each acceptable area, rent/buy choice, rooms, budget, and move-in/completion date: `[area] [whole-unit rental/shared apartment/resale/new home] [bedrooms] [price range]`. Reverse-search from the user's regular destinations: use a timed route planner to identify plausible stations/areas, then search listings there. A travel-time catchment is a discovery aid, **not proof** of a unit's commute. Keep soft features (pets, quiet, sunlight) out of the first query if they over-narrow results; verify them on retained units. Search every acceptable area independently.

## Trace the source

| Source | Useful for | Insufficient alone for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Listing portals and brokers | IDs, unit leads, photos, asking terms, contact entry | Availability, authority, full fees, physical condition |
| Owner/operator/developer pages or written reply | That publisher's terms for a particular offer | Clear title, defects, final contract or lending |
| Maps and transit operators | Route estimates, fares, named nearby places | Actual peak reliability, current shop opening, school rights |
| Current official registry/housing/policy sources | Rules and records within their scope | Private agreements and physical inspection |

Follow **lead → identifiable unit/listing ID → publisher/authority → dated price and availability → public contact entry**. Direct owners may have no corporate website; authority needs later document and identity checks. Merge duplicate postings for one unit while recording price/area/term conflicts. Do not merge different units in the same compound. Photos and floor plans must be attributable to the target unit.

## Screen and stop

Discard evidenced hard-condition failures. For viable leads, investigate move-in, total necessary cost, term/exit, actual layout, authority, and current eligibility; then perform [layout, travel, and amenity research](enrichment-guide.en.md). Protect unnecessary address/occupant details in public output. A compound page, historic sale, delisted page, generic show flat, or unverified eligibility project is a **lead**, not a contact-ready candidate.

Before stopping, report **area × platform/channel × representative query/filter × date × individual listings and unverified leads**. Pursue a potentially better low-price/shorter-commute/public option to a current entry or record why it could not be verified. Stop when further queries only repeat the same units or stale pages; “not found” does not mean “does not exist.” Recheck price, availability, and rules before the user acts.
