# Country and jurisdiction lookup: platforms, queries, and local cost fields

Use the **property's country plus state/province/UK nation/city**, not the language of the request or the user's citizenship. An English request for Tokyo uses Japanese platforms and JPY; a Chinese request for Sydney uses Australian platforms and AUD. Citizenship/residence affects eligibility, not which listing market to search. Ask only for relevant eligibility facts, not identity documents.

Read the matching row below, then [cost comparison and calculation](cost-comparison.en.md). Mainland China uses [platforms](platforms.en.md). These are starting channels, **not a ranking, exhaustive coverage, or proof of independence**; different portals may syndicate the same unit or share publishers. Open the publisher/operator's own unit page where available.

## Where and how to search

The linked platform entry pages were checked on **2026-10-19** with web reading, not end-to-end browser filter tests. UI labels can change; use visible current controls rather than inventing parameter URLs. Suggested filters below describe the task, not a guarantee every site supports every filter. Preserve native listing terms alongside the English explanation.

| Market | Rental / purchase platforms | Site actions and executable discovery query | Layout / offer trap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| United States | [Zillow](https://www.zillow.com/) for rent/buy; [Apartments.com](https://www.apartments.com/) for rentals | Rent/Buy→city/ZIP/neighborhood→maximum price, beds, baths, home type→map area→unit details/availability. Example: `site:apartments.com "Cambridge MA" "1 bed"`; purchase: `site:zillow.com "Cambridge MA" "for sale"` | Studio≠one separate bedroom; building-wide price ranges and “net effective rent”≠that unit's contractual monthly payment. Trace fee and concession terms. |
| United Kingdom | [Rightmove](https://www.rightmove.co.uk/), [Zoopla](https://www.zoopla.co.uk/) for rent/buy | To rent/For sale→postcode/town/station→radius, price, bedrooms, property type→individual listing. Query: `site:rightmove.co.uk "Reading" "to rent" "1 bedroom"`; for purchase replace with `"for sale"` | Identify England, Scotland, Wales or Northern Ireland before legal/tax checks. `pw` and `pcm` differ; “bills included” needs an inclusion list. Check whole flat vs room. |
| Canada | [REALTOR.ca](https://www.realtor.ca/) for sale/rent; [Rentals.ca](https://rentals.ca/) for rentals | City/province→For rent/For sale→map, price, beds/baths, property type→unit or MLS ID. Query: `site:rentals.ca "Toronto" "1 Bed"`; purchase: `site:realtor.ca "Toronto" "for sale"` | Basement/main-floor/full-house boundaries, den vs bedroom, parking/storage and included heat/water vary by unit. Record CAD, not an ambiguous dollar sign. |
| Australia | [realestate.com.au rent](https://www.realestate.com.au/rent/), [Domain rent](https://www.domain.com.au/rent/); use each site's Buy category for purchase | Suburb/postcode/state→Rent/Buy→price, bedrooms, property type→listing and inspection/availability information. Query: `site:domain.com.au "Parramatta" "per week"`; purchase: `site:realestate.com.au "Parramatta" "for sale"` | `per week` is not monthly. Record AUD and state/territory. A flatmate/shared-room listing does not satisfy a whole-home request. Inspection dates do not prove move-in availability. |
| New Zealand | [Trade Me Property rentals](https://www.trademe.co.nz/a/property/residential/rent), [realestate.co.nz rentals](https://www.realestate.co.nz/residential/rental); select residential sale for purchase | Region→district/suburb→rent/sale→price, beds, property type→specific listing. Query: `site:trademe.co.nz "Mount Eden" "per week"`; purchase: `site:realestate.co.nz "Mount Eden" "sale"` | Preserve NZD and quoted period. An advertised weekly rent and a tenancy's actual advance-payment dates are separate. Auction/enquiries-over prices are not agreed purchase prices. |
| Singapore | [PropertyGuru Singapore](https://www.propertyguru.com.sg/), [99.co Singapore rentals](https://www.99.co/singapore/rent); select Buy for sale | Rent/Buy→MRT/estate/district→HDB/condo/landed, whole unit vs room, budget and bedrooms→unit details. Query: `site:propertyguru.com.sg "Queenstown" "for rent" "whole unit"`; purchase: replace with `"for sale"` | An HDB “3-room” category is not a promise of three bedrooms. Read the plan and cooking/visitor/air-conditioning restrictions. A room ad cannot become a whole-unit offer. |
| Hong Kong SAR | [28Hse rental](https://www.28hse.com/en/rent), [Squarefoot rental](https://www.squarefoot.com.hk/rent); select sale for purchase | 租/rent or 買/sale→district/estate/MTR→HKD budget, bedrooms, area basis→listing/publisher. Query: `site:28hse.com "太古" "租" "兩房"`; purchase: `site:28hse.com "太古" "售"` | Saleable area (實用) and gross area (建築) are not interchangeable. Identify subdivided units and actual bedroom sizes; lift availability is not a continuous step-free route. |
| Japan | [SUUMO rental](https://suumo.jp/chintai/), [LIFULL HOME'S rental](https://www.homes.co.jp/chintai/); choose 新築/中古 purchase categories where relevant | 賃貸→prefecture/city or 沿線・駅→家賃, 間取り, 専有面積, station walk→specific building/room. Query: `site:suumo.jp/chintai/ "中野" "1LDK"`; purchase: `site:homes.co.jp "中野" "中古マンション"` | Preserve 1R/1K/1DK/1LDK and read the plan; do not translate every “1” as a Western one-bedroom. Check separate room, usable layout, guarantor and language/tenancy acceptance for the actual offer. |
| Germany | [ImmoScout24](https://www.immobilienscout24.de/), [immowelt](https://www.immowelt.de/) for rent/buy | Mieten/Kaufen→Ort/PLZ→Wohnung/Haus, price, Zimmer, Wohnfläche→listing. Query: `site:immobilienscout24.de "Berlin" "2 Zimmer" "Kaltmiete"`; purchase: `site:immowelt.de "Berlin" "Wohnung kaufen"` | Zimmer is a room count, not automatically bedrooms. Distinguish Kaltmiete (base), Nebenkosten (additional charges), Warmmiete (stated included scope); inspect kitchen inclusion and heating separately. |
| France | [SeLoger](https://www.seloger.com/), [PAP](https://www.pap.fr/) for rent/buy | Louer/Acheter→ville/code postal→appartement/maison, budget, pièces, chambres, surface→annonce. Query: `site:pap.fr "Lyon" "location" "2 pièces"`; purchase: `site:seloger.com "Lyon" "achat" "appartement"` | Pièces/T2 do not mean two bedrooms; check chambres. Distinguish HC (hors charges) and CC (charges comprises) and the actual charge list. Meublé/non meublé changes what is supplied, not proof of all-inclusive pricing. |
| Netherlands | [Funda](https://www.funda.nl/) for buy/rent; [Pararius rentals](https://www.pararius.com/apartments/nederland) | Huur/Koop→city/neighborhood→price, dwelling type, rooms, bedrooms/area where available→listing. Query: `site:pararius.com "Utrecht" "bedrooms"`; purchase: `site:funda.nl "Utrecht" "koop"` | Kamers includes rooms beyond bedrooms; check slaapkamers. Distinguish kale huur/base rent, servicekosten, utilities; preserve furnished/gestoffeerd/kaal terms and check actual inventory. |

Initial queries locate leads, not verified offers. Add pet/accessible/short-let terms in a second pass if decisive, then seek unit-specific permission and terms. For every accepted area, record query/filter, date, results, duplicates, and failed sources. If only a building/room type/starting price is available, label a lead instead of manufacturing a specific candidate.

## Local money fields to obtain, not assumed fixed rates

| Market | Monthly comparison and initial-cash checks |
| --- | --- |
| US (USD) | Contract rent vs concession-adjusted rent; mandatory amenity/service/pet charges, utility billing, parking, renter insurance. Separate application/holding fees, refundable deposit and prepaid rent; verify each fee's local legality and refundability. Purchase: property tax, insurance, HOA/condo charges, closing costs and lender quote. |
| UK (GBP) | Keep original pw/pcm; verify council tax liability/band and discounts, utilities, broadband. Separate holding payment, tenancy deposit and rent due; check whether holding money is credited/refunded before counting it again. Purchase: tenure/service charges and the relevant nation's purchase-tax regime. |
| Canada (CAD) | Utilities/heat, parking, storage, renter insurance. Deposit, advance-rent and fee rules require province-specific evidence; do not import US security-deposit assumptions. Purchase: condo fees, property tax and applicable transfer taxes plus financing terms. |
| Australia (AUD) | Normalize weekly quotes; list utilities and other necessary payments, bond and actual rent in advance separately. Check state rules and official bond process. Purchase: state/territory transfer duty and any applicable buyer-status rules, strata/council costs and finance. |
| New Zealand (NZD) | Normalize weekly rent; verify utilities/water scope, bond and rent in advance separately. Check Tenancy Services and buyer-specific purchase eligibility, rates/body-corporate charges, legal and lender costs. |
| Singapore (SGD) | Rent, utilities, internet, air-conditioning servicing and any contractually required fees; separately check deposit, agency charge and stamp duty/payment timing. HDB eligibility/permission and minimum terms need current HDB evidence; private housing uses applicable URA rules. Purchase taxes and borrowing limits need IRAS/lender/buyer-specific evidence. |
| Hong Kong (HKD) | Verify rent inclusion of management fee, rates/government rent, utilities and parking; separately record deposit, advance rent, agency charge and applicable stamping. Do not assume a standard deposit multiple or current tax rate. |
| Japan (JPY) | 家賃 plus 管理費/共益費 and relevant utilities/parking. Obtain itemized 敷金, 礼金, 仲介手数料, 保証料, insurance, lock/cleaning charges, prorated rent and renewal fees; identify refund/deduction terms. Do not apply a generic “months of rent” estimate as the actual bill. |
| Germany (EUR) | Get the base rent, service/heating advances and their scope, separately billed electricity/internet and possible reconciliation. Do not add Nebenkosten twice if already in Warmmiete. Deposit and kitchen/furniture purchase remain separate. Purchase: actual acquisition-tax/notary/registry/broker quotes and recurring Hausgeld scope. |
| France (EUR) | Separate base rent and charges; determine provision with reconciliation vs forfait, and which utilities/insurance remain outside CC. Itemize deposit, agency/inventory and signing payments; verify current applicable limits rather than encoding a universal amount. |
| Netherlands (EUR) | Base rent plus service charges and separately billed energy/water/internet; check municipal/water-board liabilities. Separate deposit, furnishings and evidenced fees. Registration permission and rental rules need municipality/current official checks, not an ad label. |

These fields are a research checklist, not legal conclusions that every charge is due or allowed. Look up the current rule **only when it affects the offer**, using the jurisdiction and contract date. Do not infer eligibility or benefits from nationality alone.

## Transport, local facilities, and official verification

For executable tools, public HTTP fallback, exact timetable/route searches and business locators, read [routing instructions](routing-guide.en.md). Do not stop at the portal homepages in the table.


Use [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps) or a locally working map for **entrance-to-entrance** routes and named businesses; read [enrichment steps](enrichment-guide.en.md). Set local weekday/arrival time and query the return independently. Then cross-check the applicable operator's fare/pass/transfer rules. A station-only journey omits both walking connections.

| Market | Useful transport and official follow-up |
| --- | --- |
| US | Find the actual city's transit agency (`[city] official transit trip planner fare`), not a nationwide fare assumption. Rental rules: state/city housing authority. Purchase finance: [CFPB home-buying resources](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/owning-a-home/); taxes/title: relevant county/state and lender documents. |
| UK | London: [TfL journey planner](https://tfl.gov.uk/plan-a-journey/) and its fares; elsewhere local bus/rail operator. [GOV.UK private renting](https://www.gov.uk/private-renting) is an England-oriented starting point; check the relevant Scottish/Welsh/NI government, not England's rules by default. Council website for council tax. |
| Canada | Relevant city/provincial operator, e.g. TTC/GO in Toronto or TransLink in Metro Vancouver; don't apply one city's pass to another. Provincial tenancy authority, e.g. Ontario Landlord and Tenant Board, for deposit/fee rules. |
| Australia | Sydney/NSW: [Transport for NSW](https://transportnsw.info/); other states their own operator. [NSW renting guidance](https://www.nsw.gov.au/housing-and-construction/renting-a-place-to-live) is only an NSW example. State revenue authority for purchase duty. |
| New Zealand | Auckland: [Auckland Transport](https://at.govt.nz/); elsewhere local operator. [Tenancy Services](https://www.tenancy.govt.nz/) for tenancy rules; relevant council and official purchase-eligibility sources for buying. |
| Singapore | [OneMap](https://www.onemap.gov.sg/) for location/routes; [SimplyGo](https://www.simplygo.com.sg/) for fares and transfer/pass terms. [HDB](https://www.hdb.gov.sg/)→Renting/Buying a Flat; search current URA, IRAS and CEA guidance for private housing/tax/agent issues. |
| Hong Kong | [MTR](https://www.mtr.com.hk/en/customer/main/index.html) plus relevant bus/ferry operator; actual paid mode and transfer matter. Rating and Valuation Department, Inland Revenue Department and Land Registry for the relevant tenancy/tax/title question. |
| Japan | Actual JR/private railway/metro/bus operator and route-specific commuter pass; check peak transfers and last services. Official MLIT/local-government tenancy guidance and qualified local advice for purchase/title/foreign-language contract issues. |
| Germany | [DB](https://www.bahn.de/) plus local Verkehrsverbund (e.g. Berlin VBB): check which services a pass covers, not just the advertised subscription price. Municipality/official law portal and the relevant state's tax office for legal/tax questions. |
| France | Paris region: [RATP](https://www.ratp.fr/) and regional fare authority; elsewhere local operator. [Service-Public housing](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/N19808) for current tenancy/purchase topics; tax authority and relevant notary quote for acquisition costs. |
| Netherlands | [9292](https://9292.nl/en/) with relevant operator/NS fare conditions; subscription coverage and bicycle access need actual checks. [Government.nl rented housing](https://www.government.nl/themes/building-and-housing/housing/rented-housing), municipality and relevant rental authority for rules. |

Transport/official links are entry points, not a claim that a specific route, fare or law has been retrieved. On 2026-10-19, static access to Transport for NSW and Tenancy Services timed out; OneMap returned no readable body. They are **unverified for actual use in that session**; follow the access-failure procedure instead of calling route research complete.

For amenities, search the actual local words as well as English: Japan `スーパー / 薬局 / 商店街`; Germany `Supermarkt / Apotheke / Einkaufszentrum`; France `supermarché / pharmacie / centre commercial`; Netherlands `supermarkt / apotheek / winkelcentrum`; Hong Kong `街市 / 藥房 / 商場`; Singapore `supermarket / wet market / pharmacy / mall`. Open a named result and route to it; do not equate a search-radius hit with convenient access.

## Markets not listed

Do not force an unsupported market into a US/UK template. Search `[city country] apartment rent buy` and the native equivalents, verify two locally relevant publisher channels and the official housing/tax/transit bodies, then record currency, rent period, room/area definitions and required cost items **before** comparing units. Say which sources/assumptions remain unverified; do not present this guide as global legal coverage.
