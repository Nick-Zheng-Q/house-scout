# Turn evidence into viewing recommendations

Do not mistake abundant data for suitability.

1. **Hard gate:** mark each hard condition met/unmet/unknown/conflicting with dated evidence. A known failure is not a usable recommendation; unknown and conflict require verification. A commute time cap must use a timed door-to-door route, not straight-line distance.
2. **Decision facts:** for each unit, obtain its own plan/floor/area definition, move-in or completion date, necessary costs, usable routes/time/fare to every destination, and named relevant daily amenities/commercial areas. If a category is missing, state whether it is a data gap or possible hard conflict.
3. **Consistent comparison:** rentals use normal monthly housing costs, signing cash, term/exit; purchases use the same price/financing assumptions and explicit scenarios; travel uses the same destination, arrival time, frequency, and feasible mode. “Known housing costs + known essential travel” is only a subtotal if utilities or route charges are unknown. Confirm whether the user's monthly cap includes travel before applying it to the combined figure.
4. **Explain preferences:** state what each remaining home gains or sacrifices in room usefulness, commute time/money, groceries/pharmacy/malls, price, and exit flexibility. If the user has not weighted preferences, do not assume cheapest wins or create an overall score.
5. **Action labels:** `worth viewing` means no known hard conflict and enough evidence to justify an on-site check—not permission to sign. `obtain evidence first` means a decisive fact is missing or conflicting. `not recommended` means a known hard failure or serious unresolved risk. Give 2–4 questions and specific routes, rooms, shops, and documents to check on-site.

## Where each answer comes from

| Question | Go to | Do not infer from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Which units and layouts are available | [Local listing platforms](platforms.en.md), operators/owners, developers, relevant public housing entries; follow the current **unit** page and plan | Compound page, generic show flat, historical sale |
| Current terms and authority | Dated publisher quote, written terms, contract; local official registry where relevant | A different unit's promotion or verbal assurance |
| Time and travel cost | Timed map route, local transit fares/timetables; separate parking/tolls and dynamic taxi prices | Straight-line distance or vehicle-only time |
| Groceries, pharmacies, malls | Named map places and walking routes, then operator/institution hours and on-site check | “Mature amenities” marketing or assumed opening |
| Title, eligibility, school, mortgage | Current local official records/rules and buyer-specific lender/professional checks | Listing badges or agent assurances |

If pages or maps cannot be accessed, use the user's materials and mark the remaining facts unverified. Do not invent minutes, fares, businesses, or rights. A compound centroid introduces route-origin uncertainty; disclose it.

If public transport is a hard requirement, a shorter walking model does not satisfy it. Follow [transit evidence checks](transit-guide.en.md); do not mark a time/fare constraint met from an untimed route, different-date reference, unsupported transfer or conditional fare alone.
